Our legacy & expertise

Established in 1973, SEN Group has grown from a single grinding unit into a leading mineral manufacturer with its own mines, five companies and advanced manufacturing units across Tamil Nadu.

Our sub-companies, including Sri Venkateswara Minerals and Sri Devi Minerals, are well known in the industry. Owning our mines ensures a steady supply of high-quality minerals.

We take pride in delivering reliable, top-grade mineral products with quality and consistency in every supply. Innovation, reliability and excellence make us a trusted name in the mineral and industrial sector.
