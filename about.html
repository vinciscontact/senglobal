Top minerals manufacturing & mining company

SEN Group of Companies, based in Chennai, is one of South India’s top mineral manufacturers. With over 50 years of experience, we are a trusted supplier of high-quality minerals used in refractories, cement, glass, tiles and ceramics.

Since 1973
Our legacy & expertise

Established in 1973, SEN Group has grown into a leading mineral manufacturer with its own mines and advanced manufacturing units.

Our sub-companies — including Sri Venkateswara Minerals and Sri Devi Minerals — are well known in the industry. Owning our mines ensures a steady supply of high-quality minerals.

We take pride in delivering reliable, top-grade mineral products — with quality and consistency in every supply. Our focus on innovation, reliability and excellence makes us a trusted name in the mineral and industrial sector.
