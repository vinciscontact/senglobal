Selective mining

We own and operate our own mines, so quality is controlled from the very first tonne of ore, with responsible extraction and minimal environmental impact.

Own mines · Tamil Nadu
02

Crushing & grinding

Ball mills, three-roller mills and mesh plants across Chennai and Salem crush, grind and screen each mineral to the exact mesh our clients specify.

Ball mills · Roller mills · Mesh plants
03

Fired at 1450°C

At Gummidipoondi, our rotary kiln calcines clay into SEN MOTTE, a high-alumina chamotte with superior thermal resistance and mechanical strength.
